Design a modern, professional, conversion-focused multi-section landing page in Arabic (RTL) for "هلا كوميرس" (Hala Kommers) — a full-service e-commerce fulfillment and operations partner for sellers across Saudi Arabia and the Gulf (GCC countries). The company handles sourcing, warehousing, order confirmation, shipping, and COD collection — acting as an all-in-one operational partner for online merchants.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
🎯 DESIGN REFERENCE — CRITICAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

The entire visual language, section structure, spacing, illustration style, and premium feel must closely match wafeq.com/ar-eg. Study these characteristics from Wafeq and replicate them with Hala's own brand colors:

- Clean, premium B2B SaaS aesthetic — "trusted enterprise" not "flashy startup"
- Generous white space between sections (80-120px vertical padding)
- Soft shadows on cards (shadow-sm default, shadow-md on hover)
- Rounded corners: 12-16px on cards, 10-12px on buttons, 8px on small elements
- Alternating white and subtle tinted backgrounds between sections
- Custom illustrations (isometric or flat style) — NO stock photos
- Heavy use of subtle decorative shapes and patterns in the background
- Typography: large, bold, confident headlines with breathing room
- Two-column hero with text on the right (RTL) and illustration on the left

✨ DESIGN PRINCIPLES (STRICTLY FOLLOW)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. BREATHING SPACE — Never cram content. Every section should feel generous and calm.
2. CONSISTENT RADIUS — Cards: 16px | Buttons: 10px | Inputs: 8px. Never mix unnecessarily.
3. SHADOWS ARE SUBTLE — Use shadow-sm by default. Reserve shadow-md for hover states.
4. ORANGE IS PRECIOUS — Use the accent orange (#E85D1F) for 5-10% of the UI only. Overuse kills its power.
5. ILLUSTRATIONS NOT PHOTOS — Every visual is a custom illustration, never a stock photo.
6. HOVER STATES EVERYWHERE — Every interactive element has a clear hover state (color shift + cursor + subtle transform).
7. ACCESSIBILITY — All text meets WCAG AA contrast. Focus states are visible. 
8. MOBILE-FIRST — Every section reflows cleanly on mobile. Stats go 2×2. Grids collapse to single column. Text scales down appropriately.
9. RTL IS NATIVE — Don't just flip an LTR design. Think RTL from the start: icons on the correct side, arrows point the right way, numbers formatted properly.
10. NO EMOJI OVERUSE — The emoji in service cards should be replaced with proper custom icons in the final design. Use emojis only as placeholders.
